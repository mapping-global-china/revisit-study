# MGC Visualization Tool — Stakeholder Review

Thank you for taking part in this session.

You will be exploring the MGC visualization tool and narrating your thoughts out loud as you work. Your reactions, questions, and observations are exactly what we are trying to capture — there are no right or wrong answers.

## Before You Start

The tool should load automatically with your session. If it prompts for a username and password, please use the credentials we sent you in a separate email.

## What to Do

1. The next step will ask permission to record your screen. When the browser asks what to share, choose **"Entire Screen"** — not "This Tab" — so your activity in the tool's tab is captured.
2. You will then see a page with a link to open the tool in a new tab. Click the link, log in, and explore.
3. **Think aloud the whole time** — say what you are looking at, what you are trying to do, and anything confusing, surprising, or interesting.
4. When done, come back to this tab and click **Next**.

Specifically, we would love to hear:
- What draws your attention first?
- What do you find yourself wanting to click or explore?
- Anything that is unclear or that you wish worked differently?

## Recording Notice

During the exploration step, we will record:
- your **screen**
- your **microphone audio**

These recordings are used for research only, stored securely, and will not be shared publicly.
