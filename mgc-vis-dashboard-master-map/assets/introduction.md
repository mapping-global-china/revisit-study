# MGC Master Map Exploration Study

Thank you for taking part in this study.

In this session, you will explore the MGC master map in your normal way and narrate your thoughts out loud while you work.

Please think aloud as you go:
- Say what you are looking at.
- Say what you are trying to do.
- Say anything confusing, surprising, or frustrating.

## Recording Notice

During the exploration step, we will record:
- your screen
- your microphone audio

These recordings are used for research only and stored securely.

When you are ready, click **Next** to continue to recording permissions.
